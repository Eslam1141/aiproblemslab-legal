AI Problems Lab Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

AI Problems Lab Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the AI Problems Lab channel to upload the channel's own videos to the owner's TikTok account.

What data the app uses

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives the permissions the owner approves: basic account information (such as display name and account ID) and permission to upload and publish videos to that account.

How it is used

Only to upload and publish the owner's own videos and to check that a post succeeded. Nothing else.

What the app does not do

It does not collect data from other TikTok users, viewers or followers. It does not sell, share or rent any data. It does not use data for advertising.

Storage

Sign-in access is held by the connection service the owner uses (Composio) and can be removed at any time by disconnecting the app in TikTok's settings or in that service.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
